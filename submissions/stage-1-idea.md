## Summary

**KaAlerto** — a community flood-intelligence and rescue-coordination app for Philippine barangays, built to keep working when internet and cellular infrastructure fail.

The Philippines takes about twenty typhoons a year. When a severe one lands, the towers congest or fall — which is exactly the hour a warning matters most, and the hour every cloud-based early-warning app goes dark. KaAlerto inverts the usual design: the phone holds its own data and computes its own map, and the server exists to help phones learn about each other rather than to decide what is true.

Reports and rescue requests travel by whatever path exists — server, then SMS, then phone-to-phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Direct. The relay only has to reach one connected phone; someone driving to higher ground carries a whole neighbourhood's data out with them.

It does not forecast. Every condition on the map was observed by a person standing there.
